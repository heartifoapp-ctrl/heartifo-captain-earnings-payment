Earnings & Payment Policy

This policy explains how Captain earnings, applicable fees, adjustments, and payouts may be handled when using the Heartifo platform.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Captain Earnings

Captains may earn money by completing eligible rides through the Heartifo platform.

The amount payable to a Captain may depend on the applicable fare structure, completed rides, promotions, platform fees, adjustments, taxes, and other applicable terms.

2. Fare Calculation

Ride fares may be calculated using factors applicable to the selected service, such as distance, duration, vehicle type, applicable pricing rules, or other platform parameters.

The fare shown in the Heartifo platform may be subject to applicable changes, adjustments, or corrections.

3. Platform Fees

Heartifo may charge or deduct applicable platform fees, commissions, service charges, or other amounts from ride transactions or Captain earnings.

Any applicable fee or commission structure should be reviewed through the information provided by Heartifo before or during participation in the service.

4. Promotions and Incentives

Heartifo may offer promotions, bonuses, incentives, or other earning programs to eligible Captains from time to time.

Each promotion may have its own eligibility requirements, conditions, duration, limits, and applicable terms.

5. Cash and Digital Payments

Depending on the Heartifo service and available payment options, Customers may pay for rides using supported payment methods.

Captain earnings may be recorded in the Heartifo system based on eligible completed rides and applicable payment rules.

6. Payouts

Eligible Captain earnings may be paid through the payout method supported by Heartifo.

Payout timing may depend on the selected payment method, banking systems, processing requirements, holidays, technical issues, verification status, and other circumstances.

7. Bank and Payment Information

Captains may be required to provide accurate payment or bank account information for receiving eligible payouts.

  • Payment information must be accurate.
  • The Captain should have authorization to use the provided account.
  • Updated bank information should be provided when necessary.
  • Heartifo may require verification before processing certain payouts.

8. Earnings Adjustments

Heartifo may make appropriate adjustments to earnings where necessary, including in cases involving cancelled transactions, duplicate payments, payment reversals, fraud, technical errors, incorrect fare calculations, or other valid adjustments.

9. Fraudulent Earnings Activity

Captains must not attempt to artificially increase earnings or manipulate the platform.

  • Fake rides or bookings.
  • GPS or location manipulation.
  • Fare manipulation.
  • Duplicate or fraudulent transactions.
  • Promotion or incentive abuse.
  • Collusion with Customers or other Captains.

Suspected fraudulent activity may result in investigation, adjustment of affected amounts, account restrictions, or other action subject to applicable law and Heartifo policies.

10. Taxes and Legal Obligations

Captains are responsible for complying with applicable tax, financial, licensing, and other legal obligations relating to their earnings and activities.

Heartifo may apply legally required deductions, reporting, withholding, or other requirements where applicable.

11. Payment Disputes

If a Captain believes that an earnings amount, payout, fee, or transaction is incorrect, the Captain may contact Heartifo Support with the relevant ride and payment information.

Heartifo may review available records and provide an appropriate resolution based on the circumstances.

12. Failed or Delayed Payments

A payout may be delayed or fail due to incorrect payment details, banking issues, verification requirements, technical problems, payment-provider issues, or other circumstances outside the immediate control of Heartifo.

13. Account Restrictions

Where permitted by applicable law, Heartifo may temporarily restrict access to certain earnings or payout features while investigating suspected fraud, disputes, verification issues, or other legitimate concerns.

14. Changes to Earnings and Payment Terms

Heartifo may update its fare structures, platform fees, incentives, payout methods, or other payment-related terms from time to time.

Applicable information will be communicated or displayed through the Heartifo platform or relevant support channels.

15. Contact Heartifo

For questions about earnings, payouts, fees, or payment issues, contact Heartifo Support.
